BUG FIX: PRODUCT COSTING AND PROFIT CALCULATION LOGIC

Current implementation is incorrect.

Problem:

When unit_type = "piece" (dona), the system is multiplying sales price by weight.

Example:

Product:
Cord Maloshni

Unit Type:
piece

Sales Price:
1500 UZS

Weight:
0.04 kg

Current calculation:

1500 × 0.04 = 60 UZS

This is wrong.

The system is treating the sales price as if it were a price per kilogram.

==================================================
CORRECT BUSINESS LOGIC
======================

CASE 1:

unit_type = piece (dona)

Sales price represents:

PRICE PER PIECE

Example:

Sales Price = 1500 UZS

Revenue from one piece:

1500 UZS

Weight must NOT affect revenue.

Weight is only used for:

* raw material calculations
* electricity calculations
* BOM calculations
* production analytics

Formula:

Revenue = sales_price

NOT

Revenue = sales_price × weight

==================================================

CASE 2:

unit_type = kilogram (kg)

Example:

Sales Price = 2.5 USD/kg

Weight = 100 kg

Formula:

Revenue = sales_price × weight

This multiplication is only valid for kilogram-based products.

==================================================
PROFIT PREVIEW FIX
==================

Current preview incorrectly shows:

Sales price × weight

For piece products.

Replace with:

If unit_type = piece:

Revenue = sales_price

If unit_type = kg:

Revenue = sales_price × weight

==================================================
PAYROLL LOGIC FIX
=================

The product form currently contains:

"Maosh stavkasi"

This conflicts with the new payroll architecture.

Implement:

Payroll Method

Options:

1. PRODUCT_RATE
2. ROLE_BASED_KG

For PRODUCT_RATE:

Salary cost comes from product payroll rate.

For ROLE_BASED_KG:

Salary cost must NOT be entered manually.

It comes from Production Line Payroll:

Producer = 1125/kg
Preparation Pool = 375/kg
Packaging Pool = 750/kg

calculated automatically by the payroll engine.

VALIDATION
==========

When unit_type = piece:

DO NOT multiply sales price by weight.

Multiply sales price by entered kg amount.
