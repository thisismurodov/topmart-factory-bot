### Replit uchun asosiy topshiriq

**Auto Shared Payroll Logic**

* Producers are paid individually by their own kg × 1125.
* Preparation workers are paid by TOTAL producer kg × 375.
* Packers are paid by TOTAL producer kg × 375.
* System automatically creates salary entries for preparation and packer workers after producer batch completion.
* Salary notifications are automatically sent to assigned Telegram accounts.
* Every generated payroll record must be stored in worker salary history.
* Dashboard must show daily, monthly and lifetime earnings per worker.

🔥 Bro, bu logika bilan maosh hisoblash deyarli to'liq avtomatlashtiriladi va eng muhimi — operator faqat ishlab chiqarilgan kg ni kiritadi, qolgan hamma narsani ERP hisoblaydi. Bu aynan tizimning kuchi. 😎🏭📦📊💰🚀
