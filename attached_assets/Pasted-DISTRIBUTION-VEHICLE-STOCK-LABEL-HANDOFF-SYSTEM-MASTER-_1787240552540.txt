6. LABEL PRINT — FIZIK HANDOFF
==================================================

mashina stockiga o'tkazib yuborma.

1. Mahsulotlar tanlanadi
2. Miqdor hisoblanadi
3. Individual labels yaratiladi
4. Label preview
5. Omborchi PRINT qiladi

7. Omborchi har bir fizik mahsulotga tegishli labelni yopishtiradi
8. Mahsulotlar agentga topshiriladi
9. Handoff tasdiqlanadi
10. SHUNDAN KEYIN stock:
   OMBOR → MASHINA

Bu ikki bosqichli nazorat bo‘lsin:

PREPARED
→ LABEL PRINTED
→ HANDED OVER
→ STOCK TRANSFERRED

mahsulot mashina stockiga o'tgan deb hisoblanmasin.
