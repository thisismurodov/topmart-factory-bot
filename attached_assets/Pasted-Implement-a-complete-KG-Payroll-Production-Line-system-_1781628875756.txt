Replace the current "Kg Maosh" settings page with a Production Line Payroll Management system that reflects the real factory workflow.

==================================
PAYROLL MODEL
==================================

There are 3 role groups:

1. PRODUCERS (Ishlab chiqaruvchilar)
2. PREPARATION (Zagatovka)
3. PACKAGING (Upakovka)

Producer payroll:
- Individual salary
- Formula:
  worker_kg × 1125

Example:
Aziza = 400 kg
Salary = 400 × 1125 = 450,000

Preparation payroll:
- Shared pool
- Formula:

(total_line_kg × 375)
÷ number_of_preparation_workers

Example:
Total kg = 1000

Pool:
1000 × 375 = 375,000

If 2 preparation workers:
375,000 ÷ 2 = 187,500 each

Packaging payroll:
- Shared pool
- Formula:

(total_line_kg × 750)
÷ number_of_packaging_workers

Example:
Total kg = 1000

Pool:
1000 × 750 = 750,000

If 2 packaging workers:
750,000 ÷ 2 = 375,000 each
