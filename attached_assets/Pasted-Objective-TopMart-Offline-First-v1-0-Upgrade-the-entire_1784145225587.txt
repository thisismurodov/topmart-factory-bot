Objective
TopMart Offline First v1.0
Upgrade the entire Field Assistant to a production-grade offline-first architecture.
The application must continue working normally when there is no internet connection.
No sales, payments, visits or new shops may ever be lost.
The ERP must eventually receive every event exactly once.
Offline mode must be transparent to the agent.
Principles
The application must become:
Offline First
Event Driven
Sync Safe
Idempotent
Production Ready
Never block the user because of missing internet.
Store first.
Sync later.

IMPORTANT
Do NOT use Service Workers for business data synchronization.
Business data must be managed through an IndexedDB event queue.
The queue is the single source of truth while offline.
ERP remains the single source of truth after synchronization.
All sync operations must be idempotent and production-safe.
Expected Result
TopMart Field Assistant should operate reliably even in villages or areas with unstable internet.
The agent must never stop working because of connectivity issues.
Every business event must eventually reach the ERP exactly once.
The user experience should feel identical whether online or offline.
