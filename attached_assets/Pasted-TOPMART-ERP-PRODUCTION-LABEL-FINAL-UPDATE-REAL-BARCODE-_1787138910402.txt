4. REAL BARCODE
==================================================

Barcode dekorativ chiziqlar BO‘LMASIN.

Real barcode generator/library ishlatilsin.

Barcode:
- scanner bilan o‘qilishi kerak;
- printerdan chiqqanda scan qilinishi kerak;
- barcode value unique bo‘lishi kerak;
- barcode value ERP'dagi konkret production record / batch bilan bog‘langan bo‘lishi kerak.

Barcode format sifatida ERP va DTP-4207 uchun eng ishonchli 1D formatni tanlang
(masalan Code 128), lekin avval mavjud stackni tekshiring.

Barcode ostida readable text ko‘rsatiladi.
